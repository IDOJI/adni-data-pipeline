# ADNI Data Pipeline

Follow a resting-state brain scan from download to analysis. Start by [setting up your study folder](RS-fMRI/docs/setup.md). Then open one step at a time.

| Step | What you get |
|---|---|
| [1 · 🔎 Choose the scans](RS-fMRI/01-selection/README.md) | A list of Image IDs to download |
| [2 · 📦 Download and convert](RS-fMRI/02-bids/README.md) | Brain images matched to their original IDs |
| [3 · 🧠 Process and check](RS-fMRI/03-imaging/README.md) | Cleaned scans and a record of which ones passed review |
| [4 · 📊 Adjust for scanner differences](RS-fMRI/04-harmonization/README.md) | A table of brain connections before and after adjustment |

Working with genetic data? Open the [SNP guide](SNP/README.md).
